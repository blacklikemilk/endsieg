The three interwar French presidential portraits were resized to the existing 156x210 leader format from historical photographs hosted on Wikimedia Commons:

- Alexandre Millerand: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alexandre_Millerand_(cropped).jpg
- Paul Doumer: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Paul-Doumer_(cropped).jpg
- Albert Lebrun: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Albert_Lebrun_1932_(2)_(cropped_2).jpg
- Paul Deschanel: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portrait_officiel_P._Deschanel.jpg

Raymond Poincaré and Gaston Doumergue use existing Endsieg portraits.
