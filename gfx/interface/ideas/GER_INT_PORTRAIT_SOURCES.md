# Weimar advisor portrait sources

The 16 `portrait_GER_INT_*_small.dds` portraits are 65 x 67 advisor cards made from the Wikimedia Commons photographs linked below. The source photographs were cropped, resized, converted to muted monochrome, and placed behind the frame and paper-note artwork from `idea_generic_political_advisor_europe_1.dds` (existing Endsieg/HOI4 artwork). These are adaptations; the photographs themselves are not included separately.

For Creative Commons photographs, credit and share-alike terms follow the linked file pages. Names shown in the credit column are those reported in Commons metadata at the time of download (2026-09-28).
