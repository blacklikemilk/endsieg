# Iran 1936-1945 focus audit — `iran_ww2`

Read-only audit of `common/national_focus/iran_ww2.txt` against the Great Game (`docs/systems/iran_great_game.md`), the Crown and Majlis balance (`docs/systems/crown_and_majlis.md`), `common/scripted_triggers/iran_continuity.txt`, `common/decisions/iran_great_powers.txt` and `localisation/english/iran_ww2_l_english.yml`.
No script was edited; this file is the only write.

## What the tree actually is

- **98** top-level focuses, not 52. Four columns: politics x0-32, economy x42-54, army x60-74, diplomacy x80-92; rows y0-13.
- 863 cost units = 6041 focus-days (~16.5 years). A 1936.1.1-1945.8 campaign affords ~3500 days, so roughly 58% of the tree is reachable in one run. The four column chains alone cost 89 (politics), 126 (economy), 85 (army), 93 (diplomacy).
- 4 roots (`IR3_inheritance_of_the_state`, `IR3_industrial_programme`, `IR3_defence_of_iran`, `IR3_between_the_blocs`), 15 leaves, max depth 14, 63 of 98 focuses at `@programme` (70 days), 11 date-gated.
- No duplicate ids, no dangling prerequisite or `mutually_exclusive` targets, no one-way exclusivity. All 98 ids have title and description localisation.
- `shared_focus = SSB_the_saadabad_pact` is declared but the tree never positions or uses it.

## 1. Thin, repetitive or generic branches

The six regime continuations are the same four-node shape (`X_wartime` → two children → `X_1945`) with the same reward skeleton:

| Branch | Wartime | Child A | Child B | 1945 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pahlavi | `IR3_pahlavi_progress` | `IR3_royal_army` | `IR3_development_corps` | `IR3_pahlavi_1945` |
| Constitutional | `IR3_constitutional_wartime` | `IR3_wartime_majlis` | `IR3_cabinet_stability` | `IR3_constitutional_1945` |
| Republic | `IR3_republic_wartime` | `IR3_republican_defence` | `IR3_civil_defence` | `IR3_republic_1945` |
| Qajar | `IR3_court_wartime` | `IR3_household_army` | `IR3_royal_finance` | `IR3_restoration_1945` |
| Nationalist | `IR3_national_mobilisation` | `IR3_oil_sovereignty` | `IR3_national_army` | `IR3_nationalist_1945` |
| Socialist | `IR3_workers_war_effort` | `IR3_tudeh_organisation` | `IR3_peoples_army` | `IR3_socialist_1945` |

- `IR3_royal_army` and `IR3_household_army` are reward-identical: army experience 25, command power, war support 0.10, plus a flag nothing reads. The crown's army and the Qajar household army are the same focus with a different name.
- `IR3_development_corps` and `IR3_workers_war_effort` are reward-identical: one instant `industrial_complex` plus one slot in state 266. The development corps and the Tudeh's shop-floor effort produce the same building.
- All six capstones run the identical six-way `remove_ideas` block and differ only in which `iran_*_enduring` idea is added plus one stat line (stability, PP or authority).
- **40 of 98** focuses hand out nothing but political power (50), stability (±0.05/0.10), war support (0.10), manpower or experience. The worst offenders are whole chains: `IR3_the_route_we_keep` (PP + stability + a tooltip), `IR3_pahlavi_1945`, `IR3_cabinet_stability`, `IR3_civil_defence`, `IR3_professional_reserves`, `IR3_conscription_expansion`, `IR3_reserve_mobilisation_plan`, `IR3_turkish_border`, `IR3_arab_neighbours`, `IR3_postwar_alignment`, `IR3_tudeh_question`.
- Politics is empty until August 1941. `IR3_succession_crisis` is `available = { date > 1941.8.24 }` and 34 focuses sit below it, so 1936-41 offers six political focuses (root, `IR3_provincial_compacts`/`IR3_direct_administration`, `IR3_name_of_the_nation`, `IR3_public_granaries`, `IR3_a_country_at_work`, and `IR3_wartime_government` from Sept 1939).
- Diplomacy is equally thin: 1936-41 is `IR3_between_the_blocs`, one of the three alignments, and `IR3_soviet_trade_pact` (which needs `country_exists = SOV`); `IR3_sovereignty_under_pressure` itself is gated on `iran_allied_pressure` or `date > 1942.12.31`.
- German economic ties, the period's real story, are `IR3_german_opening` plus two decisions (`iran_invite_german_mission`, `iran_german_barter_trade`). No industrial-mission focus, no expulsion focus, no 1941 break in the tree.
- Occupation is two focuses (`IR3_occupation_government`, `IR3_resistance_council`) and both are unreachable — see §4.2.
- The 1945 settlement tail is nine focuses in x8-10 ending in one `add_ideas = iran_settlement_enduring`.
- The five newest focuses (`IR3_zagros_fortification_line`, `IR3_reserve_mobilisation_plan`, `IR3_bandar_abbas_batteries`, `IR3_tehran_air_defence`, `IR3_corridor_supply_depots`) are one-off stat grants with no flags and no successors, appended at the end of the file.

## 4. Alternate-history gaps

1. **The DLC routing deletes 20 focuses.** `iran_route_chapter` (`common/scripted_effects/iran_campaign.txt`, the `else_if` with `has_dlc = "Graveyard of Empires"`) has a `date > 1936.6.30` disjunct in its `OR`, so with the DLC installed *every* 1936+ Iran loads vanilla `persian_focus_tree` and `iran_ww2` loads only on installs without the DLC. A Qajar restoration, constitutional monarchy, republic, nationalist front or socialist Iran therefore loses its whole wartime chapter: 20 focuses, i.e. `IR3_court_wartime`, `IR3_constitutional_wartime`, `IR3_republic_wartime`, `IR3_national_mobilisation`, `IR3_workers_war_effort` plus their three children and capstone each. The comment above the block claims the opposite intent.
2. **Occupation is dead content.** `iran_occupied` is set in exactly one place, `iran_initialize_campaign` at `date > 1941.9.16`. Nothing sets it during a campaign, so a 1936 start can never take `IR3_occupation_government` (4,7) or `IR3_resistance_council` (6,7); the 1941 crisis (`iran_campaign.40`) resolves to cooperation, resistance or transit without ever flagging occupation.
3. **A neutral Iran is never invaded.** `iran_allied_pressure` (`common/scripted_triggers/iran_campaign.txt`) needs `SOV_ultimatum_sent_flag`, `PER_has_been_invaded`, a war with ENG/SOV/RAJ, or `iran_german_alignment` plus both Allies at war with Germany. `PER_has_been_invaded` is set only inside `common/on_actions/13_goe_on_actions.txt` behind `has_dlc = "Graveyard of Empires"` — i.e. only on the installs where this tree never loads. The invasion can therefore never happen for the Iran that plays this tree unless it aligned with Germany.
4. **The transit-only outcome dead-ends.** Option C of `iran_campaign.40` sets `iran_transit_agreement` and `iran_corridor_programme` but neither `iran_allied_cooperation` nor `iran_resistance`. `IR3_persian_corridor` requires the first and `IR3_national_resistance` the second, and they are the only children of `IR3_sovereignty_under_pressure`, so the whole diplomatic branch stops there.
5. **No Axis path.** `IR3_german_opening` (92,1) leads only to `IR3_british_ultimatum_response`. There is no focus to admit German forces, join the Axis or resist the Allies with German help, and event 40 has no German option. German alignment only accelerates the ultimatum and then stops.
6. **No Iran without Reza Shah.** The politics spine is Pahlavi-shaped: `IR3_succession_crisis` has no `allow_branch` and its localisation assumes a shah, `IR3_wartime_regency` is mutually exclusive with `IR3_mohammad_reza` and open to regimes it makes no sense for, and no node covers a 1936 Iran that never crowned a Pahlavi (provisional government, officers' council). A 1936 bookmark can only ever open the Pahlavi branch: the other four flags come from interwar focuses only, so 24 focuses are unreachable without starting before 1936.
7. **The northern power is Soviet-only in name.** `IR3_soviet_trade_pact` correctly tests `country_exists = SOV`, but `IR3_azerbaijan_crisis` and `IR3_tudeh_question` test nothing but a date, `IR3_tripartite_treaty`'s `available = { is_subject = no OR = { country_exists = ENG country_exists = SOV } }` is always true, and `IR3_northern_settlement`'s only gate is `date > 1945.7.31`. Nothing in 1941-45 distinguishes a Soviet north from a Russian or an absent one.
8. **Azerbaijan and Tudeh outcomes are unread.** `IR3_azerbaijan_crisis`'s only reward is `iran_campaign.38`; `IR3_tudeh_question` gives PP and -0.05 stability. Neither leaves an outcome flag that `IR3_oil_debate` or `IR3_final_settlement` reads, so losing Azerbaijan produces the same 1945 settlement as winning it.
9. **Foreign policy never meets the settlement.** `IR3_united_nations` (82,8) and `IR3_postwar_alignment` (82,9) have no edge to `IR3_postwar_settlement` (8,10) or `IR3_final_settlement` (8,13). The ending ignores the entire diplomacy column.

## 5. Prerequisites, exclusivity and pacing

- **Chronology inversion.** `IR3_tehran_declaration` (Nov 1943) is a child of `IR3_northern_settlement` (`date > 1945.7.31`) and `IR3_regional_guarantees`. Following the prerequisites means settling the north in 1945 before declaring in 1943; avoiding that means taking the 1943 declaration with no northern settlement at all.
- `IR3_tehran_declaration`'s gate `date > 1943.11.27 OR = { has_war = no has_country_flag = iran_corridor_delivered }` is satisfied by any Iran at peace, so the date does nothing and the focus is takeable in 1942.
- **The six 1945 capstones have no date gate.** `IR3_pahlavi_1945`, `IR3_constitutional_1945`, `IR3_republic_1945`, `IR3_restoration_1945`, `IR3_nationalist_1945`, `IR3_socialist_1945` can all complete in 1937-38; `IR3_postwar_settlement` (`date > 1944.6.30`) then waits six years for its only successor.
- **`IR3_british_ultimatum_response` (92,3) is a leaf.** The ultimatum resolves and nothing follows; the corridor branch hangs off `IR3_sovereignty_under_pressure` instead, so the crisis has no consequence.
- Its prerequisite set `{ IR3_german_opening IR3_soviet_trade_pact }` is effectively German-only: `IR3_soviet_trade_pact` descends from `IR3_armed_neutrality`/`IR3_allied_cooperation`, both mutually exclusive with `IR3_german_opening`, so the second parent can never be the one that unlocks it.
- **`IR3_a_country_at_work` (8,3) is gated on an unadvertised decision.** `available` requires `iran_relief_delivered`, which only the decision `iran_bread_distribution` sets, and that decision is visible only after `IR3_public_granaries`. From the focus tree the next node simply looks permanently greyed.
- `IR3_persian_corridor` and `IR3_national_resistance` are exclusive and each needs a flag only event 40 option A/B sets — see §4.4.
- **`IR3_wartime_regency` is not guarded for non-Pahlavi.** It is mutually exclusive with `IR3_mohammad_reza`, so a Pahlavi Iran can spend 70 days to lock itself out of the young shah, and a Qajar or republican Iran is offered a regency for a dynasty it does not have.
- **Oil sovereignty is available from 1936.** `IR3_oil_sovereignty` calls `iran_reclaim_british_large` with no date or crisis gate, thirty years before `IR3_oil_debate`'s 1944-45 version.
- **The economic column cannot close early.** `IR3_economy_capstone` requires `IR3_wartime_industry` and `IR3_reconstruction_1945` (`date > 1944.6.30`) while `IR3_army_capstone` can close by 1940.
- `IR3_azerbaijan_crisis` (`date > 1945.8.1`) and `IR3_postwar_settlement` (`date > 1944.6.30`) are consistent; `IR3_tudeh_question` (`date > 1944.9.30`) precedes the Azerbaijan crisis it is supposed to precede.

## 6. Layout, computed from `x`/`y`

Grid spans x0-92, y0-13. Occupancy by row: y0 4, y1 9, y2 7, y3 8, y4 6, y5 7, y6 8, y7 15, y8 16, y9 9, y10 2, y11 4, y12 2, y13 1.

- **No overlaps and no vertical holes.** No two focuses share a cell, and every child sits exactly one row below a parent. There are no `relative_position_id` uses anywhere — all 98 positions are hand-tuned, so moving one focus desynchronises its children.
- **Worst edge: `IR3_direct_administration` (34,1) → `IR3_public_granaries` (8,2), 26 columns.** It crosses the entire politics block to join two branches that are otherwise unrelated (the other parent is `IR3_provincial_compacts` at 8,1).
- `IR3_inheritance_of_the_state` (22,0) → `IR3_provincial_compacts` (8,1) is 14 columns, → `IR3_direct_administration` (34,1) 12 columns, → `IR3_name_of_the_nation` (36,1) 14 columns. The root has no visual centre: its three children span 28 columns.
- **`IR3_postwar_settlement` (8,10) has six parents at y9 in x8, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30.** The socialist capstone's line runs 22 columns; five long diagonals converge on a node drawn under the Pahlavi column.
- **`IR3_the_route_we_keep` (16,6) fans to eight children at y7 spanning x4-x30** (26 columns from one parent). `IR3_occupation_government` (4,7) and `IR3_resistance_council` (6,7) land directly under `IR3_mohammad_reza` (0,6) and `IR3_wartime_regency` (2,6), so the occupation pair reads as part of the succession sub-branch it does not belong to.
- **The diplomacy column zig-zags 90 → 80 → 92 → 86:** `IR3_allied_cooperation` (90,1) → `IR3_soviet_trade_pact` (80,2) is 10 columns left, then `IR3_soviet_trade_pact` → `IR3_british_ultimatum_response` (92,3) is 12 columns right.
- **Mismatched AND-joins.** `IR3_gulf_service` (64,4) is joined from `IR3_airfield_network` (72,3), 8 columns away, while its other parent `IR3_zagros_defences` (66,3) is 2 away. Same pattern at `IR3_army_capstone` (60,8), 6 columns from `IR3_officer_corps_1940` (66,7).
- **Column drift.** The economy chain walks 52 → 42 across twelve rows and the army chain 70 → 60 across eight, so neither gutter between the columns is a straight channel.
- **Empty regions.** Row 0 leaves x0-20, x24-50, x56-68 and x74-84 empty. x32-40 is empty below y1, making a permanent 10-column gutter between politics and economy. x0-6 is empty below y7. x44-78 is empty below y9 except the economy tail.
- **Three different bottoms.** Below y9 there are 13 focuses: nine in x8-10 (a single-file 1945 tail four rows deep), two at y11-12 in x42-44, one at y8 in x60 and one at y9 in x82. Nothing closes on a shared row.
- **`IR3_name_of_the_nation` (36,1) is an isolated leaf** 14 columns from its parent with nothing beneath it.
- **Five single-node offshoots** hang mid-column with no sibling and no row: `IR3_zagros_fortification_line` (70,4), `IR3_reserve_mobilisation_plan` (74,2), `IR3_bandar_abbas_batteries` (68,5), `IR3_tehran_air_defence` (64,7), `IR3_corridor_supply_depots` (62,8). Their `prerequisite`/`cost` lines are also in a different order from the rest of the file.
