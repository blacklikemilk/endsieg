# Iran 1910–1918 focus tree — era audit (read-only)

Scope: `common/national_focus/iran_ww1.txt`, `focus_tree = iran_ww1`, localisation in
`localisation/english/iran_ww1_l_english.yml` + `iran_campaign_l_english.yml`, the
Great Game (`docs/systems/iran_great_game.md`) and Crown/Majlis (`docs/systems/crown_and_majlis.md`)
mechanics, `common/scripted_triggers/iran_continuity.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/iran_great_game_acts.txt`,
`common/decisions/iran_campaign.txt`, `events/iran_campaign.txt`, `interface/iran_campaign.gfx`.

Method: the file was parsed with a brace-aware splitter (not grep) for id/x/y/cost/icon/prerequisite/
mutually_exclusive/completion_reward; reachability was simulated under every mutex choice
(a prerequisite block's `focus =` list reads as OR, multiple blocks as AND); layout was computed from the
x/y grid; every sprite name was resolved against the mod and the installed vanilla `interface/*.gfx`.
No game run was performed — nothing here is engine evidence.

**Correction to the brief:** the file contains **88 focuses, not 46** (88 unique ids, 4 roots, 0 duplicate
coordinates). `iran_continuity.txt` also says "the 1910–18 chapter sets 85 outcome flags"; it sets 88.

## 4. Alternate-history gaps

The interwar chapter gates on nine route flags that **this chapter never sets** (0 occurrences of
`iran_republic`, `iran_socialist`, `iran_nationalist_front` in `iran_ww1.txt`; the six regime flags are
written only by `common/scripted_effects/iran_campaign.txt` and `iran_regime_shift.txt`):

`iran_constitutional_monarchy`, `iran_qajar_restoration`, `iran_republic`, `iran_nationalist_front`,
`iran_socialist`, `iran_pahlavi`, `iran_cossack_ascendant`, `iran_reza_khan_minister`,
`iran_jangali_congress`.

Consequences and gaps, in order of severity:

1. **No route handoff.** 1910–18 play cannot deliver a regime to 1919; the interwar settlements re-decide
   everything. Only finer-grained flags (`iran_majlis_seated`, `iran_royal_prerogative`, …) survive through
   the `iran_path_*` triggers, and those gate individual interwar focuses (12 uses), not the route.
2. **Republic: absent.** Not one focus, flag or event mentions a republic, though `IR1_assembly_of_1918`
   is the obvious seed and the interwar chapter has a full parliamentary-republic settlement.
3. **Gilan/socialist: half-wired.** `IR1_jangali_movement` sets `iran_jangali_rising`; interwar line 447
   requires `iran_jangali_congress`, which only `iran_interwar.txt` itself sets, and no focus in either
   chapter writes `iran_socialist_support` before the interwar chapter. The Jungle Movement — the single
   best 1915–18 socialist seed — feeds nothing.
4. **Constitutional centre: absent** (see `PER_bop_pull_to_centre`, §3). Qajar survival exists as a flag
   (`iran_shah_majority`) but no focus asks what kind of monarchy survives.
5. **Russian Empire collapse: absent.** 1917 is handled only by `country_exists = SOV` guards. There is no
   focus or decision on the northern vacuum — withdrawal of Russian troops from Tabriz, the Baku question,
   Persian claims in the Caucasus, or the choice between filling the vacuum and inviting the British in.
   `IR1_caspian_communications` is an infrastructure focus, not a response to the collapse.
6. **Ottoman collapse: absent.** `IR1_ottoman_frontier` and `IR1_niedermayer_expedition` cover 1914–15; the
   1917 British capture of Baghdad and the Ottoman withdrawal from the frontier (a real Persian
   opportunity and a real refugee crisis) has no focus.
7. **Other Pahlavi arrangements** are correctly out of era, but `IR1_cossack_command` /
   `IR1_officers_college` are the natural `iran_cossack_ascendant` seed and stop short of it.

**Content given to a path that should not receive it:**

- `IR1_state_bank` (18,4, `@programme`) founds a national bank in the 1910s. Bank Melli is 1927; the
  1889 Imperial Bank was British. Either re-frame as "The Imperial Bank Question" (`iran_reclaim_british_small`)
  or move it to the interwar chapter where `iran_state_bank` is already read (`iran_path_treasury`).
- `IR1_air_detachment` (46,6, `@medium`) builds an air base and grants air experience. The Iranian air force
  is 1924. Re-frame as "A Foreign Air Mission" (`iran_concede_leading_small`) or move it later.
- `IR1_jangali_movement` grants `iran_reclaim_northern_small`. The Jungle Movement fought the British in
  Gilan and the Whites on the Caspian; reclaiming *northern* influence rewards the wrong patron.
- `IR1_army_of_the_crown` requires `IR1_south_persia_rifles`, i.e. the crown's army is only reachable through
  a British-armed force — a British dependency the royalist path should be able to avoid.
- `IR1_assembly_of_1918` presents a completed 1918 assembly; the fourth Majlis did not sit until 1921.

## 5. Prerequisites, mutual exclusivity, pacing

Reachability is sound with no exclusions: 88/88. Every `prerequisite` and `mutually_exclusive` target
exists in the tree. The problems are the lockouts each choice creates:

| Choice | Also loses | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `IR1_guarded_neutrality` | `IR1_british_loan`, `IR1_south_persia_oil` | 2 |
| `IR1_entente_understanding` | `IR1_ottoman_frontier`, `IR1_niedermayer_expedition`, `IR1_tribal_frontier_pact`, **`IR1_neutrality_declaration`** | 4 (+2 mutex siblings) |
| `IR1_german_opening` | `IR1_british_loan`, `IR1_south_persia_oil`, `IR1_russian_understanding`, **`IR1_neutrality_declaration`** | 4 (+2) |
| `IR1_swedish_officers` | `IR1_cossack_pay` | 1 |
| `IR1_cossack_command` | `IR1_gendarmerie_recruitment` | 1 |

Defects in that table:

- **`IR1_neutrality_declaration` (60,2) is unreachable on two of the three diplomacy choices** because it is
  a child of `IR1_guarded_neutrality` only. Persia declared neutrality in 1914 whoever its friends were; the
  focus should hang off `IR1_between_empires`.
- **`IR1_niedermayer_expedition` (58,3) is unreachable on the Entente path** because it chains through
  `IR1_ottoman_frontier`, which needs `guarded_neutrality` or `german_opening`. Niedermayer's mission
  arrived in 1915 regardless of Persian alignment.
- **`IR1_russian_understanding` (56,2)** hangs off `guarded_neutrality` or `entente_understanding` but not
  off `german_opening`, so the German path cannot talk to Petersburg at all — the one patron it must manage.
- The German path is the shortest (loses 4 diplomacy focuses and one whole trunk) while the armed-neutrality
  path is the richest (loses 2); the three choices are presented as equal and are not.
- `IR1_army_of_the_constitution` and `IR1_army_of_the_crown` are mutually exclusive, yet
  `IR1_wartime_mobilization` (38,9) needs the *children* of both (`reserve_service` / `gulf_flotilla`) in a
  single OR block. It works, but the merge hides the exclusivity from the player: whichever army you built,
  mobilisation arrives on the same node with the same reward, so the y=7 split buys nothing below y=8.
- `IR1_the_great_famine` (20,8) and `IR1_protect_the_harvest` (66,2) both set `iran_relief_programme` and both
  fire `iran_campaign.16`; `IR1_provincial_assemblies` (4,2) and `IR1_bakhtiari_bargain` (10,2) both fire
  `iran_campaign.11`. A player can take all four and see each event twice.

Pacing: with date gates at 1914.7.31 (`IR1_wartime_mobilization`, `IR1_neutrality_declaration`), 1915.1.1
(`IR1_jangali_movement`), 1916.1.1 (`IR1_the_great_famine`), 1917.1.1 (`IR1_independence_at_the_peace`) and
1918.1.1 (`IR1_armistice_watch`), and 690 days of total content for a 3,290-day window, a 1910 start burns
its branch (≈8 days per focus) years before the gates open. The economy trunk is the worst: `IR1_the_great_
famine` sits at y=8 after eight cheap focuses and then blocks on a 1916 date, stranding the whole treasury
chain with nothing to do from mid-1911. `IR1_independence_at_the_peace` additionally requires the decision
flag `iran_relief_delivered`, so the diplomacy trunk is triple-gated (y-position, date, decision).

Also: `iran_path_foreign_count` reads `iran_foreign_paths`, which nothing in the repository writes — a dead
trigger. `IR1_majlis_budget_authority` writes a bare `iran_sovereignty = 5` where every other focus uses
`constant:iran_story.*`.

## 6. Layout

a coordinate. 101 parent→child edges; **55 of them have |dx| > 1** and 35 have |dx| ≥ 4, maximum 16.

Misplacements with meaning, not just geometry:

- `IR1_russian_ultimatum_1911` (28,2) and `IR1_dismissal_of_shuster` (28,3) are a Great Game / sovereignty
  chain drawn **inside the treasury block**, two rows under `IR1_empty_treasury` (26,0) and beside
  `IR1_domestic_workshops` (30,2). They read as a treasury sub-branch. They belong in the empty gutter at
  x=12–14, between politics and economy.
- `IR1_provincial_tax_risings` (8,3) was appended after the closing content of the file and then placed in
  the gap between the majlis column (x=0–4) and the court column (x=10); 4 columns from its parent and 2 from
  the nearest neighbour. Move to x=2, y=4 under `IR1_press_and_debate`'s row or make it a court-path focus.
- `IR1_tribal_frontier_pact` (74,3) has a dy=2 parent (`IR1_german_opening` at 74,1), leaving cell (74,2)
  empty directly above it — a hole in the outer column.
- Branch heights are wildly unequal: politics ends at y=9, economy and army at y=10, diplomacy at y=6.
  The rectangle x=43–74 / y=7–10 is 128 empty cells (the largest dead block); x=21–37 / y=7–8 is another 34;
  x=0–3 / y=7–10 is a further 16.
- Four uniform 5-column gutters (x=11–15, 31–35, 51–55, 75+) are the only separation between branches; with
  55 skewed edges crossing them the gutters do not read as separation.
- Row y=0 carries only the four roots and y=1–2 are dense; the tree is bottom-heavy on the left, empty on
  the right, which makes the diplomacy branch look unfinished next to army and economy.

## 8. Implementation package (ranked by impact)

1. **Wire the 47 orphan `GFX_iran_focus_*` sprites into the tree** (~40 of the 88 focuses) and delete the
   semantically wrong vanilla assignments in §7. No art work, fixes the largest visible-quality gap, and
   makes the chapter match the interwar/ww2 families.
2. **Make `IR1_persia_at_the_conference` a real capstone.** Add `iran_reclaim_leading_large = yes`,
   `add_to_variable = { iran_sovereignty = constant:iran_story.sovereignty_reform_gain }`,
   `PER_bop_push_majlis_small = yes`, and remove one active obligation spirit. Supporting decision
   `iran_paris_memorandum` (100 PP, 90 days, `fire_only_once`): +sovereignty, −British influence, −foreign
   leverage — the only way to spend eight years of accumulated concessions.
3. **Complete the Crown/Majlis wiring** with five effects: `IR1_royal_household` → `PER_bop_push_crown`,
   `IR1_palace_guard` → `PER_bop_push_crown_small`, `IR1_diplomatic_corps` → `PER_bop_push_majlis_small`,
   `IR1_national_gendarmerie` → `PER_bop_push_majlis_small`, `IR1_constitution_or_crown` →
   `PER_bop_pull_to_centre`. The last one is the only use of the centre tool in any chapter and makes the
   constitutional settlement playable.
