# Iran 1919-1925 - the Cossack question.
#
# Reza Khan's rise is not decided by one focus. The coup and the war ministry
# carry him past iran_story.reza_threshold on their own, which is the historical
# line; these decisions are how a player who wants a Qajar restoration, a
# constitutional crown or a republic keeps him underneath it - at a real cost in
# political power, stability and equipment, and never without a backlash.
